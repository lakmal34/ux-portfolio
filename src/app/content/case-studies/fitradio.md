# The Challenge

FitRadio had evolved over time, introducing new features and experiences without a shared design foundation. As a result, the product began to show signs of inconsistency across screens, patterns, and interactions.

## Key Challenges

- Inconsistent visual language and components
- Fragmented user experiences across workflows
- Repeated design decisions and duplicated effort
- Limited scalability for future features
- No single source of truth for design and development

The challenge wasn't simply to redesign screens—it was to create a scalable foundation that could support FitRadio's future growth while preserving its energetic brand identity.

# Understanding the User Journey

Understanding how users discover music, start workouts, and stay motivated helped identify friction throughout the experience. The redesign focused on creating a faster, more consistent journey that encourages users to stay engaged and build lasting fitness habits.

## User Needs

- Fast and intuitive navigation
- Clear workout progression
- Consistent interactions
- Motivating user experience

# Designing for Energy

Fitness products succeed when they create momentum. Every design decision focused on translating the energy of music and movement into an interface that feels dynamic, engaging, and easy to use.

## Design Principles

### Move with Purpose

Reduce friction and keep users progressing toward their workout goals.

### Create Momentum

Use hierarchy, motion, and clear actions to encourage continuous engagement.

### Amplify Motivation

Celebrate achievements and reinforce progress through visuals and interactions.

### Simplify Decisions

Present information clearly so users can focus on exercising rather than navigating.

## Visual Direction

- Bold typography
- High contrast
- Vibrant brand colours
- Dynamic layouts
- Purposeful motion
- Accessible interaction patterns

The result was an experience that felt alive, energetic, and unmistakably FitRadio.

# Building a Scalable Design System

The redesign became an opportunity to establish a shared design system that could support the product as it continued to grow.

## Foundations

- Colour system
- Typography
- Spacing
- Layout principles
- Elevation
- Motion

## Components

- Buttons
- Inputs
- Cards
- Navigation
- Modals
- Player controls
- Progress indicators
- Feedback states

## Design Tokens

- Colour tokens
- Typography tokens
- Spacing tokens
- Radius tokens
- Elevation tokens

The design system became a shared foundation that improved consistency and streamlined collaboration between design and engineering.

# Creating Consistency at Scale

A successful design system creates alignment across teams, making products easier to design, build, and maintain.

## Benefits

- Faster design iterations
- Better developer handoff
- Stronger brand consistency
- Easier product expansion
- Improved collaboration between design and engineering

Consistency became an integral part of the product rather than an ongoing maintenance effort.

# Outcome

The project transformed FitRadio from a collection of evolving interfaces into a cohesive and scalable product ecosystem.

## Results

- Redesigned the FitRadio experience around motivation and usability
- Established a unified design language across the product
- Built a scalable design system and component library
- Created a shared source of truth for design and development
- Enabled future product growth through reusable foundations and patterns

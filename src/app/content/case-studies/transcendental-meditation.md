## The Challenge

Transcendental Meditation has helped millions of people worldwide, yet the digital experience didn't fully reflect the simplicity, trust, and calmness associated with the practice. The existing website contained a large amount of information, multiple user journeys, and diverse audience needs ranging from first-time visitors to experienced practitioners. The challenge was to simplify the experience, improve content discoverability, and create a cohesive design language that could scale across the organisation's digital ecosystem.

## Understanding the Journey

Before attending a course, most visitors move through several stages.

**Curiosity** → **Learning** → **Trust Building** → **Finding a Local Centre** → **Course Enrolment** → **Practice**

The design needed to support each stage without overwhelming users with information.

## Designing for Calm

A core design principle was ensuring the interface reflected the philosophy behind the practice itself.

The experience focused on:

- Reduced visual noise
- Clear information hierarchy
- Guided content journeys
- Improved readability
- Intentional use of whitespace
- Consistent interaction patterns

Rather than competing for attention, the interface was designed to create a sense of clarity and focus.

## Building a Scalable Design System

One of the most significant parts of the project was establishing a reusable design system.

The system included:

- Design foundations
- Typography standards
- Colour tokens
- Spacing scales
- Reusable components
- Responsive patterns
- Accessibility guidelines

The goal was to create a shared language that could support future growth while maintaining consistency across the experience.

## Creating Consistency at Scale

The design system enabled:

- Faster design iteration
- Improved collaboration
- More consistent user experiences
- Easier maintenance and expansion

By moving from isolated page designs to a system-based approach, the organisation gained a foundation capable of supporting future digital initiatives.


## Outcome

The redesigned experience better aligned the digital product with the values of the organisation.

The result was a clearer information architecture, a more approachable user journey, and a scalable design foundation that supports both current and future needs.

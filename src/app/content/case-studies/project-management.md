# The Challenge

Managing projects often involves multiple teams, deadlines, and workflows spread across different tools. As projects grow, keeping everyone aligned becomes increasingly difficult, leading to missed tasks, poor visibility, and inefficient collaboration.

## Key Challenges

- Scattered project information
- Difficult task tracking
- Limited visibility into project progress
- Inconsistent team collaboration
- Complex workflows and growing project needs

The challenge was to design a flexible project management platform that helps teams stay organized, collaborate efficiently, and deliver projects with confidence.

# Understanding the User Journey

Successful project management depends on helping teams plan, execute, and monitor work without adding unnecessary complexity. Understanding how different roles interact with projects helped shape a more intuitive and collaborative experience.

## User Needs

- Clear project visibility
- Simple task management
- Effective team collaboration
- Easy progress tracking
- Flexible workflows

# Designing for Productivity

Great project management tools should help teams focus on getting work done rather than managing the software itself. The experience was designed to reduce friction while keeping projects organized and easy to navigate.

## Design Principles

### Keep Work Organized

Structure information so projects, tasks, and priorities are always easy to find.

### Improve Collaboration

Enable teams to communicate, share updates, and stay aligned throughout the project lifecycle.

### Increase Visibility

Provide clear project status, task ownership, and progress tracking.

### Reduce Complexity

Simplify workflows while supporting projects of different sizes and requirements.

## Visual Direction

- Clean and modern interface
- Clear information hierarchy
- Consistent layouts
- Flexible dashboards
- Meaningful status indicators
- Accessible interaction patterns

The result was a streamlined experience that helped teams manage projects with greater clarity and efficiency.
